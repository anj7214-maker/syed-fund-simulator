# FundOps Lab

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Users
Students preparing for fund-accounting roles and working professionals practicing operational judgment. The user approved a premium public Home page while preserving both operational workspaces.

## Product Purpose
Practice hedge-fund and real-estate fund accounting using synthetic data, connected calculations, operational exceptions and review workflows.

## Capabilities and Constraints
React/Vite/TypeScript with existing accounting engines and independent Live/Sandbox books. Home leads to separate Hedge Fund and Real Estate routes. Existing visitor registration is required for workspace entry and is not verified corporate authentication. Home demonstrations must use isolated synthetic copies, never persisted workspace books. Do not change operational navigation, calculations, access controls, Copilot or exports for this redesign.

## Brand Commitments
FundOps Lab; founded by Syed Ali Jafri ACSI ICA. Preserve the white interlocking-ring logo. Home may use midnight surfaces and restrained emerald accents; internal workspaces retain their existing institutional theme.

## Evidence on Hand
Existing synthetic books, calculation engines and prerecorded walkthroughs in public/workspace-previews. No supplied customer testimonials, conversion metrics, institutional endorsements, trial duration or paid subscription rules. Do not invent these.

## Product Principles
- Show the cause and financial effect rather than generic finance imagery.
- Distinguish an interactive demo from recorded footage and real client accounting.
- Keep the primary action asset-class neutral.
- Preserve separate Student and Professional navigation preferences without implying approval authority.
- Improve the public front door without redesigning the operating tools.

## Accessibility
Keyboard-operable controls, readable financial figures, responsive layouts and reduced-motion support. Automatic motion must have a persistent Pause control.
