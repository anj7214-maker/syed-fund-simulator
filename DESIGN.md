# FundOps Lab Design Boundaries

## Public Home
The approved public Home is a premium practice-product front door. Outcome-led brand hero, isolated interactive financial proof, two asset-class practice choices, Student/Professional pathways, four concrete capabilities, final entry action and synthetic-data disclaimer. White interlocking-ring artwork remains the product identity; its existing screen-blend treatment removes the white backdrop without editing the original image.

Midnight #070913 and charcoal #0B0F19 surfaces; crisp white headings; emerald #10B981 action buttons with dark text. Amber identifies negative hypothetical impact; success text is green. No gradient hero, decorative orbs, fabricated customer proof, free-trial claim or falsely live recording. Typography uses self-hosted, OFL-licensed Inter through the Home-only FundOps Sans family and JetBrains Mono for financial figures.

Sections are unframed bands. Only the two repeated asset choices and the genuinely framed interactive tool have card borders, at 6px radius. The primary action is asset-class neutral. The interactive demonstration uses existing engines on fresh synthetic copies; values remain readable and source explanations are disclosed on demand. On narrow phones, the impact summary precedes a one-column financial chain to avoid wrapping monetary values.

## Operational Workspaces
Existing institutional dark styles, navigation, environments, calculations and workflows remain authoritative. Home-specific classes/font family names must not be applied to those screens. Recorded walkthroughs are supporting evidence, clearly labelled, with pause and enlarged-view controls.

## Accessibility And Rollback
Keyboard controls, visible focus, reduced-motion support, stopped background/off-screen highlights and self-hosted fonts are required. `?home=classic` / VITE_PREMIUM_HOME=false restore the previous Home independently of the existing asset-class architecture rollback.

## Public Calculators
The separate public `/calculators` route preserves the existing institutional dark appearance: compact headings, restrained borders, unframed sections and tabular financial figures. The built implementation is the visual authority; no approved comp or replacement visual world is supplied. Home and operational workspace styles, navigation and workflows remain unchanged. Synthetic-data scope stays explicit; calculation and mathematical inspection are public, registration applies only to save/share/report actions, and unavailable Excel Pro details are informational, not a paywall.

Desktop places assumptions beside computed results. At 1000px and below, inputs precede results in a single column with sticky Edit inputs / View results jump links and scroll clearance; at 600px and below, narrower spacing, wrapping actions and locally scrolling tables keep figures readable without page overflow. Preserve conventional typed money/rate/integer fields, native date/select/checkbox controls and labelled model tabs. Integer-cents monetary results, reconciliation text, expandable source assumptions and mathematical traces remain inspectable; real Recharts distribution bars and NAV/HWM timelines show computed data, supported by numeric tables rather than decorative financial imagery.

Keep controls keyboard-operable with visible focus, associated labels and field errors, selected-tab state, alert/status announcements and politely announced results. Invalid assumptions pause results instead of presenting stale values as current. Retain textual warning/control states alongside color, fixed-height responsive charts with animation disabled, and reduced-motion styling; disclosures and mobile jump links must remain accessible without obscuring their targets.
