# FundOps Lab Public Home Redesign

## Approved Scope
The user approved the mentor recommendation: outcome-led hero, an isolated interactive cause-and-effect demonstration, larger Hedge Fund/Real Estate entry choices, Student/Professional pathways, founder attribution, four practical capabilities, final action and synthetic-data/access disclaimer. No operational screen or accounting engine is redesigned.

## Design Contract
Public Home is a premium practice-product front door, not a trading terminal or enterprise production-service claim. The approved composition is outcome -> interactive proof -> asset choice -> pathways -> capabilities -> action. Midnight surfaces, crisp self-hosted typography, white brand mark and restrained emerald actions; amber/red reserved for actual negative demo impacts. No fabricated customer logos, ratings, urgency, trial duration or live-data badge. Recorded video is labelled a walkthrough, not a live simulation. Default first action is asset-class neutral.

## Financial Proof
Home uses fresh synthetic baseline inputs and existing pure engine functions. Hedge Fund shows an MSFT mark change through value/P&L/NAV, including fee recalculation. Real Estate shows a valuation proposal through SPV/consolidated projected NAV. No persisted stores are imported or written, no approval is silently granted, and no actual workspace journal is posted. Source explanation remains accessible behind a disclosure.

## Boundaries And Rollback
All new presentation styles are Home-scoped. The workspace bar and pathway controls retain their existing styling and behavior. VisitorGate and workspace render callbacks remain unchanged. `?home=classic` or `VITE_PREMIUM_HOME=false` restores the previous public Home, while `?layout=legacy` retains its separate existing architecture rollback. `?previews=static` disables recorded video. No paid/free-trial promise is introduced.

Entry buttons say Explore Practice rather than promising to force Sandbox: they resume the existing workspace environment and never reset or silently switch a saved Live/Sandbox book. Conversion measurement remains an open next step; no new analytics provider or visitor-data collection was introduced and no conversion uplift is claimed.

## Validation Targets
Keyboard input, asset selection, pathway persistence, immutable demo inputs, source ties, finite numbers, reduced motion, pause/resume, video labels, mobile text fit, unchanged operational routes and production-build diagnostics. Local release is not automatically a production deployment.
